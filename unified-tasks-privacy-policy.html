Unified Tasks — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Unified Tasks is a private, personal, single-user app by James Black. It shows the user's own Google Tasks from multiple Google accounts in one widget.

What the app accesses. With the user's sign-in, the app reads the user's own Google Tasks and marks tasks complete when the user checks them off. It accesses nothing else.

Where data stays. Sign-in tokens are stored on the user's phone only. Task data is shown on the phone and sent only to Google's Tasks service, as needed to read and complete the user's own tasks. No task data is sent to any other server, third party, or service run by the app.

What the app does not do. No ads. No analytics. No tracking. No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access. The user can sign out in the app at any time, or revoke the app's access in their Google account settings, which stops all access immediately.
