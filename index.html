Full Cycle Plus — Platform Terms

Full Cycle Plus is the platform that arranges your repair. When you book here:

  • You pay Full Cycle Plus for the job. Full Cycle Plus pays the professional who does the work.
  • Full Cycle Plus assigns the professional for your job. The job agreement for this repair — the work, the price, and the warranty — is a separate agreement between you and that professional, and you sign it after booking.
  • Parts are charged at their actual cost plus Michigan sales tax, with no markup.

The job agreement link comes with your appointment confirmation. Nothing is charged here — payment is taken when the work is done, or for parts before they are ordered, as the job agreement states.
