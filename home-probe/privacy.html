Privacy Policy — My Home API Probe

Effective date: November 21, 2026.

What this app is

My Home API Probe is a personal app built for one person, James Black. It reads the on/off state of two Google Home switches (Power Saver Mode and Goodnight Mode) on his own phone, using the Google Home APIs. It is not distributed or offered to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

  • Google sign-in: your Google account identity, as provided by Google Sign-In, only to confirm which home to read.
  • Google Home device state: the on/off state of the two switches named above, read on the phone itself.

How the data is used

Only to show the state of those two switches on the phone, and to flip them when the owner taps. Nothing else.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or send your data to any server run by us — there is no server.
  • It does not share, sell, or disclose your data to any third party.
  • It has no ads and no analytics.
  • It does not read any other home, device, or account.

Data storage

Sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's phone by Google's own sign-in system. The app keeps no copy of your data anywhere else.
